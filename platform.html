02 / POLARIS

It started as a task monitor. It became the operating system of TBF.

Polaris began in 2019 with a single purpose: track what the team was working on each day. That was the whole brief. Within months it was doing more. Within a year it was doing things we had not planned for. Within three years it had become the connective tissue of the entire company.

No other company uses it. It is built entirely around how TBF works, because no off-the-shelf tool could be made to fit the combination of things we needed in one place. That specificity is also what makes it powerful.

2019
Daily task monitorThe original scope. A simple log of what the team was working on each day, replacing scattered messages and verbal updates.
2020
Hours and workload trackingEstimation data and delivery timelines meant we needed to know who was carrying what. Polaris absorbed it.
2021
Leave requests and HR flowsAnnual leave, time off, request approvals. The HR function needed a home inside the same system the team already used daily.
2022
Procurement and office requestsOrder a mouse pad, a monitor arm, lunch. Anything the team needs is requested, approved, and tracked through Polaris without a separate tool or email chain.
2023
Onboarding and trainingNew staff complete their onboarding entirely inside Polaris. Training materials, role guides, and progress tracking all live here.
2024
TBF AI assistantAsk the AI anything about the company: which tool to use, who to contact, what the process is. It knows TBF because it is trained on TBF.
2025
Tool finder and full integrationPolaris now surfaces the right Revit add-in or Genesis tool for any modeling task. Every part of daily work touches it.

04 / WHERE THIS GOES

Roadmap, not hype.
