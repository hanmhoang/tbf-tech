ABOUT THIS PAGE

Why this page exists.

This is the technology record of The BIM Factory, kept separate from our main site at thebimfactory.com. The main site is where you hire us. This is where we show our work.

For eight years we have built our own software to run BIM delivery. This page exists so clients, partners, and investors can see what that produced, in plain terms, with no sales pitch in the way.

If you arrived here from a proposal, a partnership conversation, or an investor introduction, this is the context behind it. We hold the page to three rules.

Built, not bought

Everything described here is software our own R&D team engineered, not licensed from someone else.

Live, not hype

Tools are labeled by real status: live, in development, or planned. We do not dress up what is not finished.

Numbers we can defend

We publish measured results, and we mark projections clearly as projections, not facts.

01 / THESIS

BIM delivery has structural inefficiency built into it. We chose to engineer it out rather than hire around it.

Every BIM outsourcer faces the same drag: slow estimates, repeated modeling work, contracts and invoices tracked in spreadsheets, payment status no one can see. The standard answer is to add people.

We took the other path. For eight years our R&D team has turned the repetitive parts of BIM delivery into software, owned end to end, sitting on one database. This page is the record of what that produced, and the numbers we can stand behind.
